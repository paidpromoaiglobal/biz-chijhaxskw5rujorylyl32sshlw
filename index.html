About Us

O2 Music Studio

O2 Music Studio, located in Kelambakkam on the vibrant Rajiv Gandhi Salai, is a premier music learning destination dedicated to nurturing musical talent at every level. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to refine your skills, our experienced and genuine teachers guide you from the very basics, ensuring a strong musical foundation that lasts a lifetime. We offer instruction in a wide range of instruments including violin, flute, and more, along with vocal training tailored to each student's pace and potential.

With a stellar rating of 4.9 out of 5, O2 Music Studio has earned the trust of students and parents across the region through its commitment to quality education at nominal, affordable rates. Our studio is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, making it incredibly convenient for students with busy schedules. We believe music is for everyone, and our warm, encouraging environment ensures that every learner walks away inspired, skilled, and passionate about their musical journey.
